## Why online couples therapy fits San Francisco

The Bay Area is one region pretending to be a dozen cities. A couple in Palo Alto seeing a therapist in the Mission has signed up for the 101 every Thursday. A couple in Oakland with one partner working in Mountain View has three calendars to reconcile, not two. And the work itself refuses to stay inside office hours: a raise that runs late, a launch week, a board call moved to 7pm.

Online removes the commute and keeps the session. Both of you at home after the day ends, or one of you from a hotel in New York at a Pacific-time slot that still works. No waiting room, no building anyone sees you walk into, no hour on the bridge added to a week that is already too long. [Does online couples therapy work? The research, honestly →](/blog/does-online-couples-therapy-work)

## How sessions work for Bay Area couples

**Hours.** Early morning and evening Pacific time are held for working couples, with daytime available. Because the practice also runs on Eastern and European time, a partner travelling east for the week can still join a session that lands in your evening.

**Format.** 60-minute sessions as standard, 90 minutes for the first session and for two-location sessions. Weekly or fortnightly. Both of you on one screen at home is the default.

**Intensives.** When weekly keeps failing, a two-day online intensive replaces three to six months of sessions. It suits founder couples who can protect one weekend but not fifteen Tuesdays. Yes. Founder couples are the centre of the practice: couples where one partner is building a company, and couples who run one together. Sessions are online, scheduled in Pacific time, with a free 15-minute consultation first.
