## Why online couples therapy fits New York

New York has more couples therapists per square mile than almost anywhere, and New York couples still struggle to attend therapy. The reasons are structural. A Tuesday-at-six slot in Midtown means two people leaving two offices in two boroughs, a subway each way, and a session that starts late and ends into dinner. One partner's work trip cancels it. A board week cancels it. By the third cancellation, the therapy itself has become something you fight about.

Online removes the geometry. Both of you at home, or one at home and one in a hotel, at 7:30 in the morning or 8 at night. No waiting room, no building anyone sees you walk into, no cross-town travel added to a sixty-hour week. For couples in New York, the question has rarely been whether good therapy exists nearby. It has been whether they could get to it. [Does online couples therapy work? The research, honestly →](/blog/does-online-couples-therapy-work)

## How sessions work for New York couples

**Hours.** Early morning and evening Eastern time are the busiest slots and we hold them for working couples. Daytime is available for couples who can take it.

**Format.** 60-minute sessions as standard, 90 minutes for the first session and for two-location sessions. Weekly or fortnightly. Both of you on one screen at home is the default; one of you from a hotel in another time zone is fully supported.

**Intensives.** When weekly keeps failing, a two-day online intensive replaces three to six months of sessions. Many New York couples do one intensive and then fortnightly follow-ups. [Intensive couples therapy →](/intensive-couples-therapy)

**Methods.** Emotionally Focused Therapy as the organising lens, with Gottman-derived tools where they help. [EFT explained →](/blog/emotionally-focused-therapy-explained) · [The Gottman Method and Gottman vs EFT →](/blog/gottman-method-explained-eft-vs-gottman)

## Frequently asked questions

### Do you see couples in person in New York?

No. Aether Practice is an online practice. Sessions are by secure video with both of you together at home, or in two locations when one of you is away. For couples who want concentrated work in the same room as each other, the two-day online intensive is the format, with both partners together on one screen.

### What hours do you offer for New York couples?

Early mornings and evenings Eastern time are held for working couples, with daytime available. Sessions can also be scheduled around a travelling partner's time zone, including when one of you is on the West Coast or in Europe for the week.

### Does insurance cover couples therapy in New York?

Rarely as couples therapy. Where it does, it is billed as family psychotherapy with one partner carrying a diagnosis. We are private pay; the full picture, including the five questions to ask your insurer, is in our cost and insurance guide.

### How much does couples therapy cost in NYC?

Specialist couples therapy in Manhattan and Brooklyn typically runs US$250–450 per session. Our 60-minute session is US${{price:couples-session:usd}}, private pay, with a free 15-minute consultation first.

### Can we do therapy if one of us is in New York and the other is travelling?

Yes. Two-location sessions are a standard format here, scheduled in the overlap of your time zones. Both of you need a private space and a stable connection; if that is not possible on a given day, we reschedule rather than run a session from a lobby.

### How quickly can we start?

The free consultation is usually available within a few days and a first session within one to two weeks.
