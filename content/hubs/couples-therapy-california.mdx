## Why online couples therapy fits California

California's problem is distance, in two senses. The physical one: a couple in Palo Alto and a therapist in San Francisco is a ninety-minute round trip in traffic, and a couple in Pasadena choosing a therapist in Santa Monica has made a decision about their Thursday evenings for the next six months. And the professional one: a founder in a raise, an engineer on an East Coast client, a partner who commutes to a studio or a hospital on a schedule that changes weekly.

Online therapy makes the state one place. Both of you at home after the children are down, or one of you from a hotel in New York at a Pacific-time slot that works for both. The practice is not a building in one city, which in California is the whole point. [Does online couples therapy work? The research, honestly →](/blog/does-online-couples-therapy-work)

## How sessions work for California couples

**Hours.** Early morning and evening Pacific time are held for working couples. Because we also work in Eastern and Central European time, a partner travelling east can join a session that still lands in your evening.

**Format.** 60-minute sessions as standard, 90 minutes for the first session and for two-location sessions. Both of you on one screen at home is the default.

**Intensives.** Two consecutive days online, replacing three to six months of weekly sessions. Popular with founder couples who can protect one weekend but not fifteen Tuesdays. [Intensive couples therapy →](/intensive-couples-therapy) · [Intensives for executives and founders →](/blog/couples-intensive-executives-founders)

**Methods.** Emotionally Focused Therapy as the organising lens, with Gottman-derived tools where they help. [EFT explained →](/blog/emotionally-focused-therapy-explained)

**Licensing.** Sessions are for clients physically located in California at the time of the session. If one of you is out of state for the week, tell us and we will plan around it.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do you see couples in person in San Francisco or Los Angeles?

No. Aether Practice is an online practice, which is what lets us work with couples across the whole state rather than one neighbourhood of it. Sessions are by secure video with both of you together at home, or in two locations when one of you is away. The two-day online intensive is the format for concentrated work.

### What hours do you offer in Pacific time?

Early mornings and evenings Pacific are held for working couples, with daytime available. Because the practice also runs on Eastern and European time, we can often find a slot that suits a partner who is travelling east for the week.

### Does insurance cover couples therapy in California?

Rarely as couples therapy. Where it does, it is billed as family psychotherapy with one partner carrying a diagnosis. We are private pay and provide superbills for out-of-network reimbursement where your plan allows. Employer wellbeing budgets at tech companies frequently cover therapy outside insurance; ask HR.

### How much does couples therapy cost in the Bay Area or Los Angeles?

Specialist couples therapy typically runs US$250–450 per session in both regions. Our 60-minute session is US$170, private pay, with a free 15-minute consultation first and a written Good Faith Estimate before you start.

### We're co-founders and married. Is that something you work with?

Yes, and it is a significant part of our founder work: role confusion, decision authority bleeding into the marriage, the team as audience, and the business conversation eating the intimate one. Start with the co-founder couples article, then book a consultation.

### How quickly can we start?

The free consultation is usually available within a few days and a first session within one to two weeks. Intensive dates are typically available within two to four weeks, sooner in a crisis.
